## 💡 Project Overview

### **Project Name:** SUPER — *The Sustainable Urban Planner*

**SUPER** is an interactive dashboard that integrates **NASA Earth observation data** with **community-reported insights** to guide **smarter, people- and planet-conscious urban planning**.

At its core lies the **Urban Sustainability Index (USI)** — a holistic score on a 0–100% scale derived from three sub-indexes:

| Index | Description | Data Source |
|--------|--------------|--------------|
| 🌿 **GCI** (Green Coverage Index) | Measures vegetation and land use balance | NASA MODIS / VIIRS |
| 💧 **CRI** (Climate Resilience Index) | Evaluates drought and flood risk | NASA/NOAA FLDAS & Global Flood Models |
| 🌤️ **HI** (Health Index) | Reflects air quality and land surface temperature | NASA VIIRS, ECMWF, NOAA |

### 🧭 Features
- 🗺️ **Map View:** Explore global city sustainability indicators via NASA imagery overlays.  
- 📊 **Graph View:** Visualize time-series and comparative trends.  
- 💬 **Resident Feedback:** Citizens report observations and environmental concerns.  
- 🏙️ **Leader Dashboard:** City officials respond, monitor, and plan actions.  
- ⚙️ **Supabase-Integrated Backend:** Secure data, user profiles, and feedback storage.

## 🎯 Objectives

1. **Integrate Science and Society**  
   Connect NASA Earth observation data with citizen feedback to create a shared understanding of city health.
2. **Simplify Complex Data**  
   Transform satellite datasets into intuitive visual sustainability metrics accessible to everyone.
3. **Enable Smarter Decisions**  
   Provide evidence-based tools to guide planning and improve resilience.
4. **Empower Residents**  
   Encourage participation and accountability by linking citizens and city leaders.
5. **Advance Global Goals**  
   Support **UN SDG 11** (*Sustainable Cities*) and **SDG 13** (*Climate Action*).
